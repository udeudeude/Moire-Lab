FIELD NOTES / 001

Two ways to make still pictures move

Moiré fields superimpose two regular, transparent line patterns. Their changing alignment makes large-scale waves, eddies and other apparent motion. This is the laboratory's main focus.

Barrier grids use one striped mask to reveal narrow strips of several separately drawn frames. It is a different mechanism, included for comparison and future experiments.

Drag the picture, scrub its slider or press Play. Use Download picture and Download acetate for independent, editable SVGs.
